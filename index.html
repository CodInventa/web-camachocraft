Frequently Asked Questions

Are your estimates really free?

Yes. We visit your home, measure, and give you a detailed written estimate at no cost and with no obligation.

Do you take on smaller projects?

Absolutely. From painting a single room to a complete renovation, no job is too small to do right.

Can I live in my home during the remodel?

In most cases, yes. We protect floors and furniture, control dust, and clean up at the end of every workday.

Do you speak Spanish?

Sí. Our family is bilingual, so you can talk through every detail in the language you're most comfortable with.

What does your guarantee cover?

We stand behind our workmanship. If something we installed isn't right, we come back and make it right. The details are spelled out in your written estimate.
